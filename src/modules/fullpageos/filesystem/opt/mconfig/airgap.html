Air-gapped mode

After reboot, all external network interfaces, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth are disabled. Physical keyboards are blocked. The touchscreen, on-screen keyboard, printer mainboard and USB drives remain available.

This is software-enforced isolation, not a substitute for unplugging Ethernet and removing wireless hardware where a physical air gap is required. USB drives can still transfer untrusted files.
